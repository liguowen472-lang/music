Method

How StudyTune picks music

The situation comes first. Your task, how hard it is, how tense you feel, your surroundings and whether you want music decide the category. The profile adds background context for the research. It never labels you.

SilenceWhen you would rather study without music.
Calm ResetBefore studying or on a break, when tension is high.
Soft PianoReading, memorising, writing or hard tasks.
Steady FlowProblem sets and repetitive work.
Open CanvasCreative work or low energy.

All suggestions are conservative: low volume, mostly instrumental and short. If it's noisy, try lowering the volume or moving seats rather than turning the music up.

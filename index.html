Method

How StudyTune picks music

The situation comes first. Your task and how hard it is set the cognitive load (high, medium or low). Together with how noisy your space is, that gives a shortlist of music types. How stressed you feel and what you want the music to do then tilt the pick toward calmer or more energetic music; the music goal counts about twice as much as stress. The profile adds background context for the research. It never labels you.

SilenceNo music, or very noisy with a demanding task.
Light MusicWordless and soft. Works almost anywhere.

ElectronicRhythmic tracks when you need a lift.

Harder tasks get quieter, wordless music; songs with vocals are only suggested for light tasks. If it's very noisy, we suggest changing your study environment rather than turning the music up.
